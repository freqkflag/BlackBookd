# Privacy policy (Project BlackBookd)

Project BlackBookd is self-hosted software you run on your own infrastructure. **freqkflag/BlackBookd** does not operate a hosted service or collect data from your instance unless you choose to contact us (for example via GitHub).

## Your data

All contacts, vaults, BlackBookd module data, files, and logs stay on servers you control. Administrators of your instance can access that data according to your organization’s policies.

## Third-party services

You may optionally configure email providers, map APIs, Telegram bots, object storage, and similar integrations. Those providers process data according to their own terms when you enable them.

## Security

Keep your instance updated, use HTTPS, restrict admin access, and back up your database and uploads. Report security issues via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/freqkflag/BlackBookd/security/advisories).
